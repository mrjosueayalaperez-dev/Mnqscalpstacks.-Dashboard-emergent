# WEEK 1 — Credibility & Visibility (Days 1–7)
**Goal:** Get seen. Establish that this is a real, working product with real results.

### Day 1 — Launch announcement
- **X/Twitter:** "I built a live MNQ scalping terminal — real CME feed, one-tap execution, auto risk lockouts. Here's what it looks like ↓" + 30-sec screen recording of the dashboard ticking live.
- Pin this post.
- **Discord:** Post the same video in your server + 2 trading servers you're active in (where self-promo is allowed).

### Day 2 — Testimonial / early feedback
- Post 1–2 screenshots of early user feedback (DMs, Discord messages — blur names if needed).
- Template: *"Early feedback from the first traders inside. We're keeping the group small while we scale signal delivery. [screenshot]"*

### Day 3 — Dashboard in action
- Record: open dashboard → live price → signal fires → EXECUTE → bracket attaches → FLATTEN. 45 seconds max.
- Caption: *"From signal to filled order in under 10 seconds. 40-tick stop, 120-tick target, automatic daily loss lockout at -$100."*

### Day 4 — Proof post
- Pull one screenshot from your proof gallery (the +$24.30 win or similar). Post P&L + the setup logic (EMA21/VWAP confluence).
- Always add: *"Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss. Past performance ≠ future results."*

### Day 5 — LIVE Q&A announcement + prep
- Announce a live Q&A for Day 6 in Discord + X: *"Ask me anything about the MNQ signal engine, risk rules, and how the terminal works. Saturday, [time] ET, in the Discord."*

### Day 6 — Run the live Q&A
- 30–45 min in Discord voice/stage. Screen-share the dashboard live.
- Prepared talking points: why MNQ, the 3-pillar confluence, the -$100 lockout, why you show losses too.
- End with: free tier signup link + mention Pro pricing once (no hard sell).

### Day 7 — Q&A recap + week 1 numbers
- Post recap: *"We covered X, Y, Z in the Q&A. Missed it? Highlights below."*
- Internally: log Week 1 scoreboard, note best-performing post format.

# WEEK 2 — Daily Proof & Feature Depth (Days 8–14)
**Goal:** Prove consistency. One proof post every single day + feature education.

### Daily (Days 8–14): Proof cadence
- **Every trading day:** post that day's signal outcome — win OR loss. Use the in-app **Share Card** button (branded 1080×1080 P&L PNG) — it's built for exactly this.
- Losses build more trust than wins. Template for a loss: *"Stopped out -$17.70 today. The system did its job: 40-tick stop, no revenge trade, done for the day. That's the point."*

### Day 8 — Feature spotlight: Risk Shield
- Thread/post: *"Most traders don't blow up from bad entries. They blow up from no guardrails."* Walk through: daily loss lockout, max trades/day, cooldown between orders, kill switch. Screenshots of the risk pill (SAFE / AT RISK / LOCKED).

### Day 10 — Feature spotlight: Trade Quality (signal grading)
- Explain the A+/B/C grade + confidence % on every signal card: *"Not every setup deserves your money. The engine grades each signal on 3-pillar confluence before you ever click."*

### Days 8–14 — Forum & Discord engagement (30 min/day)
- **Reddit:** r/FuturesTrading, r/Daytrading — do NOT drop links cold. Answer questions about MNQ scalping, risk management, TradingView webhooks. Link only when asked or in profile.
- **Discord:** 2–3 futures/prop-firm servers. Share one proof card/day where allowed, be helpful otherwise.
- **X:** Reply to 5 posts/day from futures trading accounts with substantive comments.

### Day 14 — Week 2 recap post
- *"14 days of posted signals: X wins, Y losses, net +Z pts. Every one posted the day it happened."* Link to the Verified Results gallery on the site.

## Non-negotiables
1. Never promise returns. Never say "guaranteed."
2. Post losses the same day as wins.
3. Risk disclaimer on every results post: *"Trading futures involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for all investors. Past performance is not indicative of future results."*
4. One CTA per post, max.
5. Track the 3 numbers daily — what gets measured gets fixed.
